## Glass Keyboard 0.1.0

- **New defaults** (taken from the author's tuned setup): number row and period key on, key opacity 65%, dispersion 10, frosted key preview, pop-up glass blur 60, animation speed 170%, opening 400 ms, "press in" key effect, no suggestion animation, "float up" key preview. Settings you changed yourself stay as they are
- **Space at the bottom of the keyboard:** a slider raises the keys, adding space between the last key row and the gesture handle
- **Backspace undoes autocorrect** can now be switched off (then backspace simply deletes)
- **Key corner radius** slider – also shapes the key preview balloon
- **Keyboard top corner radius** slider – 0 gives square top corners
- **Haptic tick** when a toolbar button is pressed
- Diagnostics section and the note at the bottom of the settings removed
